# Scylla's Band model

The model is **not** part of this archive. On the first line the add-on
downloads, from https://huggingface.co/spybyscript/scyllasband at the
revision pinned in `scyllasband_addon/pins.json` (release `v3-20261104`),
the `litert/` bundle, about 240 MB, into Subtitld's model folder, and checks
every file against its sha256.

The weights are Apache-2.0 (`LICENSE`, the model repository's). Parts of the
models derive from MIT-licensed projects, Vocos and DeepPhonemizer; their
notices are in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, also the model repository's.

The model card asks that the voices not be used for impersonation or to
attribute speech to someone who did not say it.
